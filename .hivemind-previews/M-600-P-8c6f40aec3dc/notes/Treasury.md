## Treasury Recommendations for DeFi Protocol Landing Page

### On-Chain Parameter Suggestions
1. **Transaction Fees**
   - **Parameter**: Set a dynamic transaction fee model based on network congestion.
   - **Default Value**: 0.3% of the transaction amount.
   - **Rationale**: This encourages liquidity while ensuring the protocol remains competitive with other DeFi platforms.

2. **Liquidity Pool Incentives**
   - **Parameter**: Allocate a percentage of protocol fees to liquidity providers.
   - **Default Value**: 50% of transaction fees.
   - **Rationale**: This incentivizes users to provide liquidity, enhancing the protocol's overall liquidity.

3. **Governance Token Distribution**
   - **Parameter**: Define the initial distribution of governance tokens to early adopters.
   - **Default Value**: 20% to early users, 30% for development and marketing, 50% for community incentives.
   - **Rationale**: This promotes community engagement and aligns interests between users and developers.

4. **Slippage Tolerance**
   - **Parameter**: Set a maximum slippage tolerance for trades.
   - **Default Value**: 1% for regular transactions, 3% for high volatility assets.
   - **Rationale**: This protects users from unexpected price changes during transactions.

5. **Staking Rewards**
   - **Parameter**: Establish a staking rewards mechanism for users holding the governance token.
   - **Default Value**: 10% annual percentage yield (APY).
   - **Rationale**: This encourages long-term holding and reduces market volatility.

### Operational Controls
1. **Risk Assessment Framework**
   - Implement a continuous risk assessment framework to evaluate market conditions and user behavior.
   - Regularly update risk parameters based on market volatility and user engagement metrics.

2. **Audit and Compliance**
   - Conduct regular smart contract audits by third-party firms to ensure security and compliance with industry standards.
   - Maintain transparency by publishing audit reports on the landing page.

3. **User Education**
   - Provide educational resources on the landing page about the risks associated with DeFi protocols.
   - Include tutorials on how to use the protocol safely, emphasizing the importance of understanding liquidity and slippage.

4. **Emergency Protocols**
   - Establish emergency measures for liquidity crises, including temporary fee adjustments or liquidity incentives.
   - Create a governance mechanism for the community to vote on emergency changes.

5. **Analytics and Monitoring**
   - Integrate analytics tools to monitor user engagement, transaction volumes, and liquidity pool health.
   - Use insights to adjust operational parameters and improve user experience.

### Summary
By implementing these on-chain parameters and operational controls, the DeFi protocol can enhance its sustainability, user engagement, and overall security. This will not only attract users but also build trust within the DeFi community.
