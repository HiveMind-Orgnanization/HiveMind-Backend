### Treasury Recommendations for DeFi Protocol Landing Page

#### On-Chain Parameter Suggestions
1. **Liquidity Pool Allocation**:
   - **Initial Liquidity**: Allocate 20% of the treasury to liquidity pools to ensure sufficient trading volume and user engagement.
   - **Dynamic Adjustment**: Implement a mechanism to adjust liquidity based on trading volume and user demand, reviewed quarterly.

2. **Tokenomics**:
   - **Token Distribution**: 
     - 50% to community incentives (liquidity mining, staking rewards).
     - 20% to development fund (future upgrades and maintenance).
     - 15% to treasury reserve (for operational expenses).
     - 15% to team and advisors (vesting schedule over 2 years).
   - **Burn Mechanism**: Consider a periodic token burn (e.g., quarterly) to reduce supply and increase scarcity.

3. **Governance**:
   - **Voting Rights**: Implement a governance token that allows users to vote on treasury management decisions, including fund allocation and protocol upgrades.
   - **Threshold for Proposals**: Set a minimum threshold (e.g., 5% of total supply) for governance proposals to ensure active participation.

#### Operational Controls
1. **Risk Management Framework**:
   - **Audit Requirements**: Ensure all smart contracts undergo third-party audits before deployment to mitigate security risks.
   - **Insurance Protocols**: Consider integrating with DeFi insurance protocols to cover potential losses from smart contract exploits.

2. **Treasury Management**:
   - **Diversification**: Maintain a diversified treasury portfolio, including stablecoins, governance tokens, and other DeFi assets to mitigate volatility.
   - **Regular Reporting**: Establish a quarterly reporting process to review treasury performance, liquidity status, and risk exposure.

3. **User Engagement Incentives**:
   - **Referral Program**: Implement a referral program rewarding users for bringing new participants to the protocol, funded from the community incentives pool.
   - **Staking Rewards**: Offer attractive staking rewards to encourage long-term holding and participation in governance.
