### Treasury Recommendations for Chess Game Development

#### On-Chain Parameter Suggestions
1. **Funding Allocation**
   - Allocate budget for development phases:
     - **Phase 1 (Setup)**: 20% of total budget for initial setup costs (infrastructure, tools).
     - **Phase 2 (Frontend Development)**: 30% for UI/UX design and implementation.
     - **Phase 3 (Backend Development)**: 30% for API and database integration.
     - **Phase 4 (Testing and Deployment)**: 20% for quality assurance and deployment costs.

2. **Transaction Fees**
   - Set a transaction fee cap of 1% for any in-game purchases or premium features to ensure affordability while maintaining revenue.

3. **User Account Management**
   - Implement a fee of 0.5% for account creation to cover operational costs, with a cap on the maximum fee to avoid deterring users.

#### Operational Controls
1. **Budget Monitoring**
   - Regularly review budget allocations against actual expenses to ensure adherence to financial plans.
   - Implement a quarterly financial audit to assess spending efficiency and adjust allocations as necessary.

2. **Risk Management**
   - Establish a reserve fund of 10% of the total budget to mitigate unforeseen expenses or development delays.
   - Monitor market trends in gaming and user engagement to adjust the budget dynamically based on user acquisition and retention rates.

3. **Performance Metrics**
   - Define KPIs for financial performance, such as:
     - User acquisition costs.
     - Revenue per user.
     - Retention rates post-launch.
   - Use these metrics to inform future funding rounds or adjustments in operational strategy.

4. **Compliance and Security**
   - Ensure all financial transactions comply with relevant regulations (e.g., GDPR for user data).
   - Implement secure payment processing systems to protect user financial information.

### Delegation
- **Financial Analyst Agent**: Task with ongoing budget monitoring and financial reporting.
- **Compliance Officer Agent**: Ensure adherence to legal standards and regulations throughout the development process. 

By implementing these treasury strategies, we can ensure the chess game project is financially sustainable and positioned for success.
