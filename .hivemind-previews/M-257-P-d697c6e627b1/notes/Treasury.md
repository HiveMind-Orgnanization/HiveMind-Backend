### Treasury Recommendations for Snake Game Development

#### On-Chain Parameter Suggestions
1. **Budget Allocation:**
   - **Frontend Development:** 40% of total budget
   - **Backend Development:** 30% of total budget
   - **Testing & QA:** 20% of total budget
   - **Contingency Fund:** 10% of total budget

2. **Transaction Fee Management:**
   - Set a maximum transaction fee threshold (e.g., 0.5% of total budget) for API interactions to ensure cost efficiency.

3. **Revenue Model:**
   - Consider implementing in-game ads or optional premium features to generate revenue post-launch.

4. **High Score Data Storage:**
   - Allocate a portion of the budget for secure data storage solutions (e.g., MongoDB) to ensure player data integrity.

#### Operational Controls
1. **Budget Monitoring:**
   - Implement a monthly review of budget utilization against the allocated percentages to ensure adherence to financial plans.

2. **Risk Assessment:**
   - Conduct a risk assessment for potential overspending on development phases, especially in frontend and backend integration.

3. **Audit Trails:**
   - Maintain detailed logs of all financial transactions related to the game development to ensure transparency and accountability.

4. **Compliance Checks:**
   - Regularly review compliance with data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR) to mitigate legal risks associated with player data.

5. **Performance Metrics:**
   - Establish KPIs to measure the success of the game post-launch, focusing on player engagement and revenue generation.

#### Delegation
- **Development Oversight:** Assign a project manager to oversee the development phases and ensure budget adherence.
- **Financial Audits:** Delegate a financial officer to conduct regular audits of expenditures related to the game development.

By implementing these parameters and controls, we can ensure the financial viability of the Snake game project while minimizing risks.
